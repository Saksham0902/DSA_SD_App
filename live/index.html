From Rubber Duck Dojo

Out-loud practice for SDE interviews

Get asked. Answer out loud. Get faster.

A mock call where an interviewer asks you DSA, concept and system design questions. You answer out loud, and the app times how fast you start, checks which key ideas you hit, and schedules the weak ones again.

1 · Hear it

The interviewer reads the question aloud, like on a real call. Ask a clarifying question if you need one.

2 · Say it

Start talking. The clock measures how long you take to begin, and lights a dot for each key idea you mention.

3 · Fix it

The debrief shows what you missed and the model answer. Rate yourself and the app brings it back at the right time.
